# H5 — Research Question

**Stage:** `H5_MULTI_PAIR_USD_MOMENTUM_VOLATILITY_DESIGN`
**Experiment:** `QPF-RV-2026-05-H5-MULTI-PAIR-USD-MOMENTUM`
**Clock:** internal ordinal only (`NOT_UTC`)

---

## The question

> When multiple USD-oriented FX pairs show **directionally coherent recent movement** and realized
> **volatility is elevated** relative to each target pair's own trailing history, does the direction
> of that coordinated USD move have a **statistically positive continuation tendency** over one or
> more subsequent H1 horizons?

## Scope and meaning

- H5 is a **distinct directional-predictability hypothesis** — it is **not** a replacement
  interpretation of the rejected cointegration studies (EURUSD/GBPUSD, AUDUSD/NZDUSD, EURUSD/USDCHF
  H4). Those outcomes close only their specific formulations.
- The hypothesis concerns **coordinated USD direction + volatility condition + subsequent H1
  continuation**.
- A positive result would be **statistical evidence only**. It would **not** demonstrate
  profitability, net PnL, tradability, or permission to trade.
- Outcomes are **future log-price changes conditioned on historical information** — **not** strategy
  returns and **not** PnL.
- **No result may be used to select pairs, thresholds, or horizons after the fact.**
- H5 explores a **limited, pre-declared grid** and confirms only a **small number of selected
  candidates once on sealed data**.
- This is a **predictive-statistical** hypothesis, **not** an executable trading strategy. No cost,
  spread, commission, slippage, swap, financing, fill, latency, position-size, stop-loss, portfolio,
  or execution conclusion is allowed.
